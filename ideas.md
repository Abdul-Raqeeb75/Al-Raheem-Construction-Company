# Reference-Grounded Redesign — Warm Property Editorial

## Ground-Truth Reference

The supplied reference image is the visual specification for this redesign. The construction website will adopt its warm off-white editorial canvas, compact white header, wide rounded image hero, layered proof panel, asymmetric project-image collage, and dark/light split About composition. The existing Al-Raheem Construction logo, project content, genuine testimonial data, and core interactions remain, but the prior blue/navy card-heavy composition is replaced.

## Style Decisions

**Color philosophy.** Use warm cream as the main canvas, walnut brown and muted charcoal as grounding colors, and restrained sand-gold for premium calls to action. Blue is no longer a dominant brand surface.

**Layout paradigm.** Use a vertical editorial page rhythm with one wide rounded hero, an overlap-proof card, a mosaic project gallery, a split About block, and a contained testimonial panel.

**Signature elements.** Rounded architectural imagery, floating proof cards, sand-gold buttons, and asymmetrical photo arrangements appear throughout.

**Typography.** Use `DM Sans` for readable navigational and body content, paired with `Playfair Display` only where a refined editorial headline needs emphasis.

**Interaction.** Hovering over work tiles reveals a concise project action. Buttons lift subtly. The testimonial carousel maintains its pause-on-hover behavior.

**Brand voice.** Direct, composed and craft-led. Example: “Built around the way you live.” and “Discuss your next build.”

**Lower-page refinement.** Contact and footer moments must carry the same composed editorial confidence as the hero: clear next-step language, walnut and sand-gold material contrast, and a visible Al-Raheem signature rather than generic utility styling.

**Image discipline.** Portfolio and current-project imagery should share a warm architectural grade that favors natural materials, practical construction detail and restrained color, even where project categories differ.

**Proof integrity.** Visible testimonials and project claims remain limited to supplied, genuine information. Existing pending feedback states stay neutral rather than inventing client language.
